carries NO license disclosure anywhere, and shows placeholder
contact details.

     dre_license_number
     brokerage_name
     brokerage_dre_number
     agent_name
     contact_email
     contact_phone
     business_address

   Never hardcode any of these in a component.

     {agent_name} · DRE #{dre_license_number}
     {brokerage_name} · DRE #{brokerage_dre_number}

   visible build-time warning in development and log an error in
   production. A missing license number must never fail silently.
