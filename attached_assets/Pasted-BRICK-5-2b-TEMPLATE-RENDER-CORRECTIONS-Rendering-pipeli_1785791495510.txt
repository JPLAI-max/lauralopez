BRICK 5.2b — TEMPLATE RENDER CORRECTIONS

Rendering pipeline works. These are geometry and data fixes.
Do not change the scrim approach, font loading, or focal crop.

1. ADDRESS FIELD IS STREET ONLY — highest priority
   The address layer is receiving the full address with city,
   state, and ZIP. It overflows the canvas and is clipped on
   both sides, and it duplicates the city already shown in the
   subline directly below.

   Feed the address layer the STREET LINE ONLY — e.g.
   "12 N MAPLETON DR", not "12 N MAPLETON DR, BEVERLY HILLS,
   CA 90210". Parse it off the properties.address field: take
   everything before the first comma. If there is no comma, use
   the whole string.

2. FIT-TO-WIDTH ON ALL TEXT LAYERS
   No text layer may EVER exceed 84% of canvas width. Measure
   the rendered width and step the font size down in 2px
   decrements until it fits, to a floor of 60% of the specified
   size. Apply to headline, address, and subline.

   Clipping at the canvas edge is never acceptable output.
   Enforce this in the renderer, not per-template.

3. HEADLINE MAX WIDTH
   Replace the 18-character rule with a width rule: shrink from
   72px until the headline fits 84% of canvas width, floor 52px.
   Still never wrap to two lines. Character counts are a poor
   proxy — "SPECTACULAR VIEW ESTATES" is 24 chars and nearly
   spans the full canvas.

4. RULE SPACING
   The divider currently overlaps the address ascenders. Move
   the rule to y 17.2% and the address to y 20.2%. Subline
   moves to 22.6%. Verify visible clear space above and below
   the rule.

5. BOTTOM SCRIM STRENGTH
   Increase the bottom gradient: start at 72% of height instead
   of 78%, and end opacity 0.55 instead of 0.42. The signature
   block must stay legible over a bright subject — test against
   the sunset photo with the pool reflection.

6. SIGNATURE BLOCK SPACING
   Increase separation: brokerageMark y 87.5%, roleLine y 91.0%,
   agentName y 94.2%. The three lines currently read as one
   compressed block.

7. RE-RENDER AND COMMIT
   Regenerate all six preview PNGs with these corrections and
   commit them to /docs/template-previews/, overwriting the
   existing files.

   Add one more: render with the LONGEST street address in the
   seeded properties and save as
   /docs/template-previews/long-address.png to prove fit-to-width
   holds.

ACCEPTANCE
1. No text touches or crosses the canvas edge in ANY preview.
2. Address line shows street only, no city or ZIP.
3. Visible clear space above and below the rule.
4. Signature legible over the bright pool area of the sunset
   photo.
5. All seven PNGs committed.
