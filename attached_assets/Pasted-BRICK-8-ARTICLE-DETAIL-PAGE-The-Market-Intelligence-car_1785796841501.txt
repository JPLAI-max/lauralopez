BRICK 8 — ARTICLE DETAIL PAGE

The Market Intelligence cards render with cursor-pointer and
"Request Full Report" text but have no Link, no onClick, and no
handler. There is also no article detail route registered in
App.tsx. The card is decorative.

1. ARTICLE DETAIL ROUTE
   Register /intelligence/:slug in App.tsx (public, outside the
   admin router). Build pages/ArticleDetail.tsx.

   Fetch from the existing GET /api/content/articles/:slug.
   Do NOT add a new endpoint.

   Layout: hero image from heroMediaId when present, category
   label, title, published date, then rendered markdown body.
   Match the editorial feel of the rest of the site — generous
   measure, serif body, no sidebar.

   Published date must be visible. Dated content is the whole
   credibility claim.

   404 state for an unknown slug. A draft article must 404 on
   the public site — verify this, it was acceptance check 13 in
   Brick 4.

2. MAKE THE CARDS LINK
   Wrap each card in a Link to /intelligence/{slug}. Change the
   label from "Request Full Report" to "Read" — the article is
   free to read and the current label misdescribes it.

3. GATED REPORT CTA — separate from the articles
   At the bottom of the Market Intelligence page, add a distinct
   section: "Request the Quarterly Market Report".

   It opens the existing inquiry form with inquiryType
   preset to "Market Report Request" and the message field
   prefilled with the report name. Reuse ContactForm — do not
   build a second form, and do not create a second submission
   path.

   The intelligence opt-in checkbox appears as normal,
   unchecked. Requesting a report is not consent to a list.

4. ADD THE INQUIRY TYPE
   Add "Market Report Request" to the inquiryType allowlist on
   BOTH client and server. The server whitelist is in
   routes/inquiries.ts — they must match or submissions 400.

ACCEPTANCE
1. Clicking a Market Intelligence card opens the article at
   /intelligence/{slug} with full body text.
2. Published date renders on the detail page.
3. An unknown slug renders 404, not a blank page.
4. A draft article 404s on the public site.
5. The quarterly report CTA opens the inquiry form with the type
   preset and submits successfully.
6. The new inquiry type appears in the admin Inquiries list.
7. Article detail readable at 375px.
