Laura Lopez Website Design Prompt

Project Overview

Design a luxury advisory website for Laura Lopez, Director of Beverly Hills Estates.

The website should feel like a hybrid of:

Beverly Hills Estates
A private wealth advisory firm
A family office
An architectural journal

The site should inherit the sophistication and credibility of Beverly Hills Estates while establishing Laura’s distinct identity as an advisor to multi-generational and high-net-worth families.

This is NOT a traditional luxury real estate website.

The objective is to communicate:

Intelligence.
Discretion.
Legacy.
Market expertise.
Strategic thinking.
